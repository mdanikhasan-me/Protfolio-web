# Design direction

Working concept: **Massive Builder Workshop — from pixels to systems**

The site will feel like an editorial workshop rather than a literal factory or software dashboard.
Warm paper, carbon ink, cobalt, oxidised teal, restrained vermilion, and small safety-yellow signals
create one material system. Measurement marks, build stamps, pixel units, assembly paths, and
practical diagrams explain how raw parts become finished products.

The first viewport must communicate Anik's website and custom software offer without motion or
metaphor. Motion remains short, optional, and local to the pages where it adds meaning. Mobile
receives its own composition. Reduced-motion mode remains complete and intentional.

The system excludes glassmorphism, generic dark-tech gradients, floating orbs, fake terminals,
dashboard navigation, bento layouts, fake statistics, copied agency effects, custom cursors, sound,
autoplay media, and game-like navigation.
