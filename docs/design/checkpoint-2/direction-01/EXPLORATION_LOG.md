# Signal Weave exploration and rejection log

This is the small process record required for Checkpoint 2. Heavy outputs remain ignored in
`.local-validation/`; the final review material is the deterministic SVG set in this folder.

## Explored variants

| Variant                                                 | Medium                      | Decision                                       | Reason                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Single braided monogram                                 | Pencil/SVG logic            | Rejected                                       | It became a logo-like central object and risked Website 01/03 recognition                                                 |
| Four straight “data cables”                             | SVG                         | Rejected                                       | It read as generic technology decoration and did not provide chapter transformation                                       |

## Built-in image-generation exploration

Mode: built-in ImageGen, abstract material/atmosphere study only. No generated image is committed or
presented as a final asset. Local output:
`.local-validation/concept-renders/signal-weave-imagegen-exploration.png`.

Prompt:

> Macro study of four continuous round mineral-ceramic filaments under controlled tension, braided
> across a midnight-indigo frame with cyan, saffron, berry and violet accents; matte granular
> surface, grazing light, calm asymmetric opening; no text, UI, logo, person, triangle, angular
> ribbon, grid chamber, iridescent glass, neon panel, or capsule.

Inspection: the output respected the round, mineral, non-logo intent and produced a useful granular
surface cue. Its crossings were far denser than the concept can tolerate and several filaments
competed equally. The generated composition was rejected. The final board keeps four clearly
separated paths, a protected reading interval, and flatter lighting.

## Blender exploration

The reproducible script at `tools/blender/checkpoint2_system_studies.py` rendered
`.local-validation/blender/signal-weave-study.png` in Blender 5.2.0 LTS. It confirmed that:

- round curves can cross without producing the angular ribbon silhouette;
- the strongest distance read comes from open spans, not a dense central knot;
- close material should use controlled grazing light and stronger base colour than the first neutral
  render;
- four procedural curves remove the need for a heavy GLB.

The `.blend` and PNG remain local. They are feasibility evidence, not selected public art.

## Refinement performed after inspection

The selected SVG system reduces crossing density, places the aperture off-centre, keeps project
media as a labelled pending slot, makes mobile a new vertical composition, and uses role changes
rather than rotations or colour-only variants.
