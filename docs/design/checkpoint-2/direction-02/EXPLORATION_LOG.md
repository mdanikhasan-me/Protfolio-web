# Kinetic Ledger exploration and rejection log

Heavy experiments remain ignored in `.local-validation/`. The final review boards are deterministic SVG
using repository typography.

## Explored variants

| Variant                                                  | Medium                    | Decision                                        | Reason                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Liquid word filling a rounded media window               | Motion sketch             | Rejected immediately                            | It reproduced Website 02's capsule-and-melt relationship                                                                |
| Editorial list with occasional large type                | SVG                       | Rejected                                        | It became beige editorial repetition with no governing transformation                                                   |
| Equal spreadsheet rows and numeric counters              | SVG                       | Rejected                                        | It read as a cyberpunk/corporate dashboard and invited fake metrics                                                     |
| Paper bands pinched at the exact centre                  | Built-in image generation | Rejected as composition; material note retained | The fibre and ink were useful, but perfect central symmetry made the result static and ornamental                       |
| Unequal full-viewport strata with a moving pressure seam | SVG                       | **Selected for Direction 02**                   | It creates a typographic spatial system, keeps evidence clear, needs no central object, and has the lowest runtime cost |

## Built-in image-generation exploration

Mode: built-in ImageGen, abstract paper/pressure study only. No generated letters or production
imagery were permitted. Local output:
`.local-validation/concept-renders/kinetic-ledger-imagegen-exploration.png`.

Prompt:

> Layered chalk paper and opaque cobalt, vermilion, plum, yellow and near-black ink strata under one
> precise vertical pressure event; horizontal bands locally compress and recover; no text, letters,
> UI, logo, human, capsule, liquid drip, green-black palette, dashboard, or floating panel.

Inspection: the tactile paper and clear colour separation support the material direction. The centre
pinch was too symmetrical, the bands were too equally weighted, and the visible “crease” could
become a decorative fold rather than state. The generated composition was rejected. The final board
moves the seam, varies band hierarchy, and binds it to navigation and project state.

## Blender decision

Three layered-slab ideas were considered and rejected. They added a central sculptural object,
lighting cost, and a false need for 3D. Because typography and semantic HTML are the concept's
actual strength, Blender is explicitly not used for this direction.

## Refinement performed after inspection

The selected system caps deformation to a narrow influence zone, preserves an accessible semantic
heading, gives media a full-width evidence band, redraws the rule for mobile, and reserves obvious
elastic behavior exclusively for this concept.
