# Checkpoint 2 concept comparison

This comparison describes trade-offs; it does **not** select a winner for Anik. Scores are
directional design judgements at concept stage, not measurements. A five-point strength score is
used for positive criteria; cost/risk rows use **Low / Medium / High** with a short explanation.

## One-line distinction

- **Direction 01 — Signal Weave:** continuity and refinement expressed through one uninterrupted
  bundle that changes tension and role.
- **Direction 02 — Kinetic Ledger:** evidence and implementation pressure expressed through a
  typographic register with no central 3D object.
- **Direction 03 — Constraint Field:** many independent implementations becoming coherent through
  visible environmental rules.

## Useful labels without choosing for the user

- **Strongest overall balance:** Signal Weave. It combines identity, continuity, evidence framing,
  mobile fallback, and a credible Play seed with fewer authorship risks than the field.
- **Most technically practical:** Kinetic Ledger. Its primary visual form is semantic HTML/CSS and
  its rich effect can remain a small optional layer.
- **Boldest environmental direction:** Constraint Field. It replaces the hero object with a complete
  rule-driven environment and lets project imagery act as a force.
- **Highest originality-control risk:** Kinetic Ledger, because its permitted elastic typography
  must never drift into Website 02's font, capsule, viscous shapes, timing, or composition.
- **Highest implementation risk:** Constraint Field, because an authored deterministic environment
  can easily degrade into generic particles without strong tooling and performance gates.
- **Best Play-route foundation:** Constraint Field and Signal Weave are both strong for different
  mechanics. Constraint Field offers constraint placement and pulse routing; Signal Weave offers
  path topology and limited rerouting. Neither requires Website 04's character or world.

## Project-image transformation comparison

All directions reserve truthful `[PROJECT IMAGE — PENDING]` regions until approved assets arrive:

- Signal Weave samples approved media colours into the nearest strand segments and changes local
  tension around the aperture.
- Kinetic Ledger transfers approved media colours into adjacent rules while the evidence band takes
  structural ownership of the page.
- Constraint Field treats approved media as a boundary condition: nearby marks turn, clear space,
  and receive a distance-faded palette.

No direction fabricates a screenshot or infers unfinished project imagery.

## Decision questions for review

1. Should Anik's first impression feel tactile and continuous, typographic and precise, or
   environmental and exploratory?
2. Is the future Play connection a quiet seed or a major identity obligation?
3. Does project evidence need the clearest possible flat register, or should it visibly transform
   the surrounding world?
4. Which system still feels recognisably Anik when motion, WebGL, and project imagery are removed?
