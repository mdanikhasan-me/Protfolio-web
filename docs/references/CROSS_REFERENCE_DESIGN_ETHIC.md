# Cross-reference design ethic

- Checkpoint: 1 research synthesis
- Status: principles only; not an approved art direction

## Central finding

The references do not succeed because they use many effects. Each succeeds by maintaining a small
number of stable rules while changing the visual world:

- Website 01 separates an immersive selection chamber from conventional evidence pages.
- Website 02 gives one media aperture several reversible roles and confines deformation to a local
  threshold.
- Website 03 coordinates object, copy, contrast, and tempo as shared chapter state.
- Website 04 separates normal portfolio, playable world, and archive fallback while preserving an
  exit and access to the work.

The transferable ethic is **continuity with restraint**: one original transformation rule, one
recognizable identity system, readable evidence, and motion that communicates state.

## Required design principles for later concepts

1. **One original signature rule**

   A later concept may use an object, material, field, typographic transformation, or spatial rule,
   but it must be invented for Anik and perform more than one meaningful role.

2. **Spectacle is not the evidence**

   Project facts, status, technologies, screenshots, source/release links, and calls to action must
   stay accessible as HTML. Motion may establish focus, context, or transition; it cannot substitute
   for proof.

3. **State must be legible**

   Users should know whether they are on a normal page, entering a project, opening an immersive
   layer, or starting Play. Controls, back/close behavior, focus, and URL/history must agree.

4. **Reversibility**

   A strong entry transition also needs a deliberate exit. The user must be able to close rich
   media, restore the previous context, and use normal navigation without replaying a sequence.

5. **Motion has a job**

   Allowed jobs include establishing hierarchy, focusing selection, showing causality, confirming
   proximity, preserving continuity, or reorienting after a mode change. Motion with no state or
   communication purpose should be removed.

6. **Different chapters may have different tempos**

   Home can be expressive, Work comparative, About human, Stories readable, Contact calm, and Play
   exploratory. Shared identity comes from the rule set, not identical section templates.

7. **Rich media is isolated**

   Normal content routes must remain lightweight. The separate Play route may own its own bundle,
   controller, assets, and fallback. A teaser on a normal page must not pull in the game.

8. **Fallback is a first-class state**

   Reduced motion, no WebGL, context loss, save-data, low-power devices, keyboard-only use, and
   mobile input need complete, truthful alternatives—not a blank canvas or fake unsupported banner.

9. **Generated art cannot become fake evidence**

   Original abstract art, textures, and game assets may later be generated. Project UI, screenshots,
   metrics, testimonials, clients, awards, and product results may not be invented.

10. **Identity remains Anik’s**

    The factual center is a software-focused student, solo founder building Boilabin, and practical
    developer learning by building. Reference aesthetics cannot overwrite that identity with an
    agency, celebrity designer, AI lab, or game-studio persona.

## Candidate motion vocabulary, not an art direction

The following categories are available for later original concepts:

- bounded object/material transformation;
- shared-element expansion/contraction;
- clipped threshold deformation with recovery;
- chapter contrast transition;
- focus through depth or scale;
- proximity light/particle feedback;
- documentary/evidence handoff;
- calm reset and exit.

No later concept should use all of them. A coherent concept should choose a small subset and define
duration, trigger, reduced-motion behavior, and performance boundary.

## Information architecture ethic

- Home: establish identity and route to work/services without requiring interaction mastery.
- Work: provide both a curated entry and a complete conventional index.
- Project pages: lead with truthful status and evidence, then optional experiential media.
- About: replace abstraction with human context and verified facts.
- Stories: prioritize reading; use at most one bounded expressive transition.
- Contact: direct, familiar, calm, and truthful about response paths.
- Play: separate route, explicit opt-in, visible controls, progress, pause/exit, and fallback.

## Performance ethic

- Budget normal pages and Play separately.
- Do not keep invisible canvases, videos, or decoders active.
- Cap device pixel ratio and post-processing.
- Load only the selected project media.
- Prefer transforms and bounded masks over full-screen repaints.
- Preserve a semantic DOM beneath any visual duplicate.
- Detect capability honestly and test the fallback, not only the best GPU.
- Treat one desktop recording as inspiration, never as performance evidence.

## Accessibility ethic

- Familiar link and button semantics remain visible.
- Motion cannot be the only signal of focus, selection, progress, or proximity.
- Readable HTML survives effect failure.
- Reduced motion removes deformation and long camera travel without removing content.
- Keyboard and touch paths are designed, not retrofitted.
- Fullscreen and pointer capture are voluntary and escapable.
- Focus returns to the initiating control after closing an immersive layer.

## Boundary before Checkpoint 2

This synthesis does not select a visual identity, signature object, palette, type system, hero,
game, or public copy. Checkpoint 2 must produce three genuinely different original directions after
this audit is approved.
