## Reading

Website 04 contains three related but distinct products:

1. a conventional, bright portfolio whose persistent animated robot acts as guide;
2. an explicitly entered third-person discovery world;
3. a separate weekly-idea archive with nodes and readable project cards.

The close frames correct a shallow “3D robot portfolio” reading. Work, About, and Contact remain
readable interface compositions around the character. The playable world has its own movement
instructions, discovery progress, compass, quality mode, and exit route. When the world becomes
unsupported, the site explicitly announces an archive fallback instead of hiding the content.

## Playable-world close observations

- Movement instructions are visible at the bottom: WASD/arrows, Shift to run, drag to look, and
  approach a beacon to read it.
- The discovery counter and compass make progress and direction explicit.
- The camera is not fixed: height, pitch, and distance change across frames, consistent with damped
  third-person follow plus user look input.
- The terrain uses a very dense field of long blades. Foreground blades can fill the screen and
  temporarily obscure the character and target.
- Vertical warm/cool light columns and a glowing diamond distinguish destinations from the grass.
- The Quality/Performance toggle is not ornamental. The surrounding frames show a short black/render
  interruption consistent with switching a render preset or recreating resources.
- The reference demonstrates a need for camera collision/occlusion management; it does not prove
  that the original solved every case.

## Archive close observations

- The fallback banner remains visible throughout the archive capture.
- A central introductory state explains the collection before navigation.
- Luminous nodes vary in form—small squares, orbs, and ringed markers—along a spatial line field.
- Approaching/selecting a node opens a conventional evidence card at the right. This is the correct
  place for readable project information; the star field supplies scale, not the facts.
- The vertical week indicator and previous/current-week controls give a non-spatial orientation
  layer.
- Frames with extreme bloom occur when the camera/scroll passes close to a node. The visual effect
  communicates proximity but can overwhelm content and must be capped in any original system.

## Performance and fallback implications

- Normal pages should not load the game bundle. The reference’s clear mode boundary supports this.
- A production third-person web scene would require compressed GLB, texture budgets, instancing,
  frustum/distance culling, device-pixel-ratio limits, adaptive grass density, camera collision,
  suspend-on-hidden, and a tested context-loss path.
- The archive can be far cheaper than the grass world if its line/node background is bounded and its
  evidence cards remain DOM.
- A static/list archive is not an error page; it should expose every item, normal links, search, and
  keyboard access.
- The capture proves one desktop appearance only. It does not prove the stated quality toggle’s
  exact behavior, mobile controls, integrated-GPU performance, save-data handling, or accessibility.
