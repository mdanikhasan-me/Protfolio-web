# Internal linking map

## Commercial paths

- Home links directly to the website and custom software service pages through the primary actions
  and service section.
- Each service page links to `/hire/` with a service query and to the most relevant proof.
- `/hire/` links to `/contact/`; `/contact/` routes visitors back to the right context.
- Native Windows service content links to the SoctuKit case study and vice versa.

## Project and writing paths

- `/work/` links to all four verified project entries, including the separate lab route.
- Boilabin links to the seller-interest page; that page links back through breadcrumbs.
- SoctuKit links to native Windows service and the public release repository.
- UIU Discord Bot links to custom software and its related first-hand article.
- Salty Potato AI links to the first-hand language-model article and the article links back.
- Writing pages link to an author page, a related project, and the writing index through
  breadcrumbs.

## Supporting discovery

- Global navigation exposes Home, Services, Work, Writing, About, and Hire Me.
- Global footer exposes Now, Open Source, Résumé, Contact, and Privacy.
- Search indexes public content but is itself noindexed and excluded from the sitemap.
- The 404 page links to Home, Work, and Search.

Every important route must remain reachable through an ordinary anchor without requiring client
JavaScript.
