## What I can build

I take on focused website projects where the job is clear and the finished site needs to feel
considered. That can mean a business website, an ecommerce site, a product or startup site, a
portfolio, a web application interface, or a careful redesign of an existing site.

The work begins with structure. I map what a visitor needs to understand, what the site needs to
prove, and what action should follow. That keeps the visual work connected to a real purpose and
gives search engines complete, meaningful pages to crawl.

## How I approach a website

### 1. Define the useful pages

We identify the audiences, important questions, proof, and conversion paths. I avoid thin pages
created only for keywords and keep ordinary navigation available at every step.

### 2. Build a strong static foundation

Where the project allows it, I favour semantic HTML, server- or build-rendered content, small
client-side scripts, responsive images, and predictable links. The website should still explain
itself when animation or JavaScript is unavailable.

### 3. Add a visual system that belongs to the product

Typography, spacing, colour, media, and motion should support the subject. I do not start from
effects and then search for somewhere to use them.

### 4. Verify the release

Before handoff, I check the production build, page metadata, keyboard paths, responsive layouts,
forms or their fallback, and the routes a visitor is most likely to follow.

## Performance and search foundations

Good technical SEO is part of the implementation: distinct page titles, useful descriptions,
canonical URLs, crawlable links, sitemap coverage, structured data that matches visible content, and
a page hierarchy a person can understand. Performance is treated the same way. Images receive
dimensions and responsive sources, fonts stay local, and interaction code is loaded only where it
earns its cost.

None of this replaces useful writing or real reputation. It gives those things a sound technical
surface.

## A good fit

This service is a good fit when you have a real business, product, or professional goal; can provide
the facts and media needed to represent it honestly; and want someone to think through both the
interface and the implementation.

It is not a marketing-agency package, mobile app service, or promise of rankings. If the main
challenge is custom operations rather than a public website, the
[custom software service](/services/custom-software-development/) may be the better path.

## Common questions

### Can you work with an existing website?

Yes. A project can be a focused improvement or a full replacement, depending on what the current
site needs and what must remain compatible.

### Do you build ecommerce websites?

Yes, when the required catalogue, checkout, operational, and content scope can be defined clearly. I
am also building Boilabin, but I do not publish private implementation details from that product.

### Can you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Dhaka and open to suitable remote projects outside Bangladesh.

### What do you need to estimate the work?

A useful first brief explains the website's goal, important pages or features, available content,
intended timeline, and any systems it must connect to. A budget range is helpful but optional.
