## What Boilabin is

Boilabin is a pre-launch ecommerce startup for Bangladesh. It is a real product under active
development, not a portfolio mockup, and I am building it independently as its solo founder.

The initial model is deliberately controlled: begin as a curated retailer, source and sell selected
products, and take responsibility for the product selection, presentation, platform quality, and
customer experience. The immediate work is earning trust, not opening the doors as widely as
possible.

## Why I am building it

Boilabin is my main entrepreneurial direction. It brings product decisions, interface quality,
commerce, operations, and long-term ownership into one difficult system. That makes it different
from a client project or a short technical exercise. I have to think about what the product should
be, what should wait, and what must remain private before launch.

## The current product direction

The public direction is a curated ecommerce experience for Bangladesh. I want product selection and
presentation to feel deliberate rather than like an uncontrolled listing feed.

After the platform has earned trust and a useful audience, the longer-term plan is an
invitation-only seller programme for selected emerging brands with genuinely good products and good
branding. It is planned, not open. There is no general seller registration and no expression of
interest can promise acceptance.

## What remains private

I do not publish Boilabin's private code, system architecture, suppliers, operational detail,
unreleased features, customer data, or launch planning. I also do not claim a launch date, revenue,
customers, sellers, market share, funding, partnerships, a team, or delivery coverage that has not
been verified.

That boundary is part of the case study. Pre-launch work should be discussed honestly without
turning private implementation into marketing content.

## Current status

Boilabin remains under active development. The useful public next step for a brand is to read the
[planned seller model](/boilabin/seller-interest/). For website or software work, Boilabin is
