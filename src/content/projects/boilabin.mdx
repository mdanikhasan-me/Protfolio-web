## What I work on most

Boilabin is an online marketplace for Bangladesh. I started it, I built it, and I run it.

I built all of it: the store people shop in, the backend and database, accounts, listings, orders, and the admin tools for running it day to day.

## After launch

A feature isn't finished when it goes live. Once people use it, I find the awkward parts, the cases I missed and the slow pages. I keep fixing them.

I can't show the code, customer data or supplier details here. They're private.

## What's next

I'm still improving it: new features, faster pages, a smoother experience. I'm also thinking about an invite-only seller program for a few selected brands. It won't be open to everyone.
