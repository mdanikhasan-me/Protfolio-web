## The product I spend the most time on

Boilabin is a Bangladesh-focused ecommerce marketplace I founded, built, and currently run. It is
the project that ties together most of what I care about: product decisions, interface quality,
commerce, operations, and the less visible systems that keep all of those pieces connected.

I built the product front to back. That includes the storefront people browse and buy through, the
backend and database behind it, and the operational side: accounts, listings, order flow, and the
admin tools used to manage the product day to day.

## Building it is only half the job

Running a marketplace means the work does not stop when a feature ships. Real use exposes awkward
interactions, missing edge cases, and performance problems that were easy to miss in development. I
keep working through those details instead of treating the first functional version as finished.

The public case study stays focused on what I can show honestly. Private code, customer data,
suppliers, and operational details remain private.

## What comes next

Boilabin is still being improved. I am adding features, tightening performance, and refining the
experience as the product grows. A future invitation-only seller programme is also being considered
for selected brands; it is not a general seller-registration promise.
