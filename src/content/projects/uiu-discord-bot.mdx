## Notices come to you

UIU posts its notices on a public notice board, and you have to keep going back to check it. The bot does the checking. It looks for new notices on a schedule and posts them in a Discord channel you choose.

It remembers what it already posted, so nothing appears twice, and new notices come in order. It also shows the academic calendar and has a few server commands.

## Made for UIU students

It's written in Python with discord.py.

It's not an official UIU service. It never logs in to UCAM and never asks for your university password.
